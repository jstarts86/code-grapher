2. Final Report (Individual Submission)


The report must be submitted individually and should contain the following sections:

Part A: Shared Team Information (May be written collectively)

- Project Name and Subject.

- Full technology stack used during implementation.

- Overall project structure and features.

- Detailed feedback received from the final corporate demonstration.

- Evidence photos from the final demonstration.

Part B: Individual Contributions (Must be written personally)

- Structure and Features: Detailed explanation of the specific parts you developed.

- File Locations: List the file paths and names you worked on. Clearly distinguish between individual and joint files.

- Demonstration Media:
	- Screenshots of the specific parts you developed.

	- A link to a YouTube video demonstrating your individual contributions.


- APIs: A list of Open APIs used (if applicable).

- Reflection: Personal thoughts on the project development process.

- Feedback Reflection: Individual summary of the corporate feedback and your thoughts on it.

Software Engineering Tips for this Report:

- Technical Stack Justification: When listing the stack, briefly note why a specific technology was chosen (e.g., "Used React for component reusability" or "Used FastAPI for high-performance asynchronous handling").

- Clean Documentation: In the "File Locations" section, consider using a tree structure or grouping files by module to demonstrate a solid understanding of the project's architecture.

- Impact over Task: In your reflection, focus on the challenges you overcame and the architectural decisions you made. This shows growth as an engineer rather than just a list of tasks completed.
